Awesome Mix 1.1.1

- Speed dial no longer slips back to Quick picks or your library after a while. YouTube Music's web home moves its account-headed shelf (the one under your account name) between positions; Awesome Mix now finds it in any position, and reads up to two more pages of the home feed when the first page has none.
- A good speed dial is never replaced by a fallback: the last one taken from your listening history is kept (also across restarts) and shown whenever a refresh only finds Quick picks or the library. Quick picks and the library are used only on a first start with nothing kept. Signing out forgets it.
- Pin to speed dial: press X (or hold) on a tile and choose "Pin to speed dial". Pinned tiles always come first, in the order you pinned them, survive every refresh and restart, and "Unpin" removes them.
- NowPlaying.log says which home shelf was chosen (its position and why), how many continuation pages were read, and when the last good set was kept instead of a fallback.
