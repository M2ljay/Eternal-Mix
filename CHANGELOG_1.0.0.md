Vol. 1 (1.0.0)

First release as Awesome Mix (Eternal-Mix), the renamed Now Playing Plus fork of Now Playing 2.5.2 by Andrea Sgarro (LoZazaMastro).

- New plugin folder "Awesome Mix". On first start the settings of "Now Playing Plus" are copied over once (see README, "Moving from Now Playing Plus").
- Versions are shown as volumes: 1.0.0 is Vol. 1.
- Carries every Now Playing Plus change: YouTube Music playback fixes, podcasts with resume and Continue listening, seeking, playback speed, Steam theme matching, steadier audio output.
