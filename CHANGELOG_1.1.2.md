Awesome Mix 1.1.2

- Speed dial no longer turns into a seasonal shelf (such as Christmas music). A home shelf now counts as your speed dial only when it is really yours: its header line is your account name or its picture is your account photo, or it is YouTube Music's own Speed dial or Listen again shelf (known by its name or its pin entries). A shelf that merely has a header line, as seasonal and editorial shelves do, never qualifies.
- A speed dial saved by 1.1.1 is discarded once, as it could hold such a shelf; the next good one is saved again.
- NowPlaying.log names the chosen shelf's title, so it is clear what was picked (your account name is never written).
- Episodes for later: when YouTube Music answers in a shape Awesome Mix cannot read, the list is empty instead of failing, and any such error shows a short line ("YouTube Music couldn't load this right now") instead of the raw parser text. The log keeps a shortened technical line.
